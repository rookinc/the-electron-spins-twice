\section{The certified \texorpdfstring{$\operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]$}{AT4val[60,6]} carrier}

The argument begins with a finite graph, not with a physical model and not
with an interpretation of a drawing.  The graph is fixed before the spiral,
the inverse construction, the signed lift, or the double-rotation claim is
introduced.

\begin{definition}[The registered carrier]
Let
\[
    V_{60}=\{0,1,\ldots,59\}.
\]
The \emph{registered carrier} \(G_{60}=(V_{60},E_{60})\) is the simple
tetravalent graph whose canonical 120-edge labeling is carried by the
bundled certified payload
\texttt{data/g60\_local\_edges.csv}.  As an unlabeled graph,
\[
    G_{60}\cong \operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]
    \cong \operatorname{Graph52002}.
\]
The symbol \(\operatorname{Graph52002}\) denotes the House of Graphs
identifier.  The notation \(\operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]\) is the GraphSym
tetravalent arc-transitive census identifier.
\end{definition}

The canonical payload has 120 distinct unordered pairs, uses every vertex
of \(V_{60}\), contains no loops, and gives degree four at every vertex.
Consequently,
\[
    |V(G_{60})|=60,
    \qquad
    |E(G_{60})|=120,
    \qquad
    \deg(v)=4
    \quad
    (v\in V(G_{60})).
\]
These are carrier data.  They are not conclusions inferred from the
geometry developed later.

\begin{proposition}[Native Cayley presentation]
In the standard action of \(A_{5}\) on
\(\Omega=\{0,1,2,3,4\}\), set
\[
    x=(0\,1\,3),
    \qquad
    y=(1\,4\,2).
\]
Then
\[
    x^{-1}=(0\,3\,1),
    \qquad
    y^{-1}=(1\,2\,4),
\]
and the registered carrier has the exact presentation
\[
    G_{60}
    \cong
    \operatorname{Cay}
    \bigl(
        A_{5},
        \{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\}
    \bigr).
\]
The four cross-products between the two inverse pairs have order five, and
the four commutators determined by the two inverse pairs have order three.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The certified order-60 transport audit constructs a regular action on the
sixty vertices and identifies its regular group with the standard
alternating group \(A_{5}\).  Under the exported vertex-to-group bijection,
the four neighbors of the identity are exactly
\[
    S=\{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\}.
\]
Direct multiplication in \(A_{5}\) verifies the four connection-element
orders, the four cross-product orders, and the four commutator orders.
Transport by left multiplication then reproduces every one of the 120
edges of the canonical carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Native four-state deck action]
For the connection set
\[
    S=\{x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\},
\]
the connection-set stabilizer in \(A_{5}\) has order four and satisfies
\[
    N_{A_{5}}(S)\cong V_{4}.
\]
Its action agrees with the native four-state deck action of \(G_{60}\).
The corresponding quotient has fifteen vertices and is intrinsically
isomorphic to the line graph of the Petersen graph:
\[
    G_{60}/V_{4}\cong L(\operatorname{Petersen}).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The \(A_{5}\)-conjugacy orbit of \(S\) has size fifteen.  Orbit-stabilizer
therefore gives a stabilizer of size four.  The certified permutation
action identifies its three nonidentity elements as involutions, hence the
stabilizer is \(V_{4}\).  Its orbits on the sixty carrier states coincide
with the native four-element fibers.  The induced fifteen-vertex quotient
has the certified adjacency relation of \(L(\operatorname{Petersen})\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Full carrier symmetry]
The full automorphism group of the registered carrier has order \(480\).
More precisely,
\[
    \operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
    \cong
    \left\{
        (\sigma,d)\in S_{5}\times D_{8}
        :
        \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)=(-1)^{\chi(d)}
    \right\},
\]
where \(D_{8}=\langle r,f\mid r^{4}=f^{2}=1,\ frf=r^{-1}\rangle\) and
\[
    \chi(r^{i}f^{j})=i\pmod 2.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})\) is the fiber product of \(S_{5}\) and
\(D_{8}\) over \(C_{2}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The explicit certificate exports four generators of the native
automorphism group and matches them with four generators of the displayed
fiber product.  Extension by group words produces a bijection on all 480
elements.  The certificate checks all \(480^{2}=230400\) ordered products,
so the exported bijection is an isomorphism rather than an order
coincidence.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The \(G_{60}\)--\(G_{900}\) boundary]
The identifiers \(\operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]\) and
\(\operatorname{Graph52002}\) name the sixty-vertex carrier \(G_{60}\).
They do not name the later 900-state closure.  The object \(G_{900}\) will
be constructed from fifteen registered \(G_{60}\) fibers together with a
separate gluing law.  The equality \(900=15\cdot60\) is a state count, not
an identification of graphs.
\end{remark}
