## Finite twist register

The signed twist coordinate is

    q in {-2,-1,0,1,2}
    theta = q*pi

The states q equals plus or minus 2 are first full-turn limits. The states q
equals plus or minus 1 are half-turn states. The equilibrium is q equals zero.

The bare orientation quotient identifies q equals -2, 0, and 2, but the signed
receipt distinguishes the lifted states.

## Typed events

Orthogonal passage holds q fixed while transmitting the orientation receipt.

Nonorthogonal drive advances q toward the limit with the sign of the arriving
action. The first limits saturate at plus or minus 2.

Passage and release cannot share one untyped zero input. Passage holds every
state, while release moves every nonzero state toward equilibrium.

## Restoration jurisdiction

The selected author-supplied jurisdiction is

    G900: plus or minus 2 -> plus or minus 1
    G15:  plus or minus 1 -> 0

The finite operator permits the role-swapped assignment as an abstract
factorization. The operator alone does not select the named jurisdictions.

## Perfect emission and backaction

At the half-turn state, a registered 180-degree emission is the typed release
event. It triggers the G15 backaction that closes the state to equilibrium.

Given the equilibrium target, the required backaction is unique:

    +1 -> 0 requires -1
    -1 -> 0 requires +1

The emission is not counted as an additional scalar torsional action term.
This avoids double counting and does not assert a continuous momentum law.
