# Lawful informative action orientation contract 185

## Status

This is an author-supplied candidate construction contract. It is not an
archive-recovered theorem and does not revise the negative result in Artifact
184.

## Author semantics

If external action is encountered, G900 orients G60 orthogonally to the
action, and the action proceeds through the structure in accordance with the
incidence of the arriving action.

## Relational orientation law

Let the two-element G60 orientation torsor be

    {H, H_inverse}.

Let an admitted arriving action a carry ordered incidence. The candidate
framing map is

    F: A_lawful -> {H, H_inverse}

with reversal covariance

    F(a_inverse) = inverse(F(a)).

The native mirror remains fixed at offset 4. Conditional target readout is

    forward arriving incidence -> H         -> 4 to 1
    reverse arriving incidence -> H_inverse -> 4 to 2

An action without directed incidence selects neither orientation.

## G900 operation

The proposed operation has four stages:

1. Admission
   Determine whether the arriving action is lawful.

2. Framing
   Place the native G60 mirror axis orthogonally or complementarily to the
   arriving incidence under an exact pairing that remains to be defined.

3. Orientation
   Use the direction carried by the admitted incidence to select H or
   H_inverse.

4. Transport
   Propagate the action through G60 only along incidence supported by the
   arriving action, preserving its required receipt.

## Relationship to Artifact 184

Artifact 184 proves that the closed internal archive does not choose between
H and H_inverse. This contract does not contradict that result. It proposes
that the missing arrow is supplied relationally by an external admitted
action.

## Open construction gates

The following remain unconstructed:

- the exact lawful-action domain;
- the admission predicate;
- the arrival-incidence schema;
- the combinatorial meaning of orthogonality;
- non-degeneracy of the orientation pairing;
- uniqueness for every admitted action;
- reversal covariance on native action records;
- the incidence-supported transport operator;
- receipt preservation.

No unconditional offset-1 selector or physics claim is made.

## Keeper

The mirror belongs to G60. The arrow belongs to the action. G900 must
lawfully join them.
